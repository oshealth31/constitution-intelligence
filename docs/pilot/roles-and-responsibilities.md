# Founding pilot: who owns and is responsible for what

The positions Constitution Intelligence proposes to take in the pilot agreement. They are
**business proposals, not legal advice**. Every point marked ⚖️ must be reviewed by a
qualified legal professional in Botswana before any agreement is signed (full list in
[templates/pilot-agreement-requirements.md](templates/pilot-agreement-requirements.md)).

| Matter | Constitution Intelligence | The organisation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform ownership** | Owns the software, its design, the Constitution Intelligence name and brand, and all improvements, including ones suggested by pilot organisations. Grants the organisation a right to use the service during the pilot; nothing is sold or transferred. | Gets no rights in the software beyond use during the pilot. |
| **Content ownership** ⚖️ | Owns none of the organisation's content. Uses it only to provide the service to that organisation: storing it, extracting text, searching, showing it to the organisation's members. Never shows it to another organisation, never uses it for marketing without written permission, never uses it to train AI. | Owns its constitution, documents, logo, approved answers, simple-words texts and member list. Confirms it has the right to upload them (see the submission process). Is responsible for the **accuracy and approval** of everything published to its members. |
| **Service access** | Provides the portal at `https://<address>/o/<organisation>` for the pilot period, to the number of users in the pilot plan (currently up to 100 members, 3 documents, 200 MB of files). May suspend access for non-payment (after the grace period), misuse, or a security emergency, with notice where possible. | Decides who gets access, and with which role. Keeps its accounts to real people and removes people who leave. Does not share passwords. |
| **Support obligations** | Support by email and WhatsApp during **working hours** (Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 Botswana time), first response within **1 working day**, **4 working hours** for "portal down". Best effort, no guaranteed uptime during the pilot. Onboarding help: one setup session and one training session per organisation. | Names one **pilot contact** who reports problems, collects members' questions and joins the feedback sessions. |
| **Data protection** ⚖️ | Proposed: acts as **processor** for members' personal data (names, email addresses, roles, activity), on the organisation's instructions. Keeps it secure (encryption in transit, encrypted backups, access controls, audit log), in South Africa or Botswana only (see the open hosting question), reports breaches to the organisation without delay so the 72-hour notice to the Commission can be met, and helps with members' requests. | Proposed: acts as **controller** of its members' personal data: decides who is added, tells members their data is used for the portal (privacy notice), has a lawful basis, and handles members' requests with Constitution Intelligence's help. Church organisations: membership may reveal **religious belief**, which is sensitive data. |
| **AI limitations** | AI answering is **switched off** for the pilot. Members get: the constitution itself, search, "Find relevant clauses" (word matching, no AI), and answers and simple-words texts written or checked **and approved by the organisation**. Drafts may have been prepared with AI help before upload; they are labelled and must be approved by a person. Nothing in the portal is legal advice. | Reads and approves every answer and simple-words text before members see it. Accepts that the original constitution wording is what counts. |
| **Pilot duration** | **60 days** from portal activation. At the end: a completion review, then the organisation chooses to continue on a paid plan, or leave with an export of its data. | Takes part in a mid-pilot check (day 30) and the completion review (day 55–60). |
| **Payment and cancellation** ⚖️ | Founding pilot fee: **from P1,500 per organisation** for the 60 days (amount set per organisation in the agreement and on the invoice; never hard-coded). Paid by EFT or mobile money against an invoice; access starts when payment is **verified**. Proposed: cancellation by either side with 7 days' written notice; refund rules to be decided ⚖️. | Pays the invoice by its due date, using the invoice number as the reference, and reports the payment in the portal (Manage → Subscription). |
