# Template: confirmation that an organisation's data was deleted

Send to the Owner (or the person who asked for the deletion) after the deletion is complete.
Fill in the brackets from the Control Centre's "Data deleted" card. This is a template, not
legal advice: have the wording checked with the pilot agreement.

---

**Subject:** Your data has been deleted from Constitution Intelligence

Dear [name],

As you asked on [date of the written request], we have deleted [organisation name]'s data from
Constitution Intelligence. The deletion was completed on [date and time, from the card].

**What was deleted**

- Your constitutions, every version and clause, and the documents you uploaded ([number] files)
- Approved answers, simple-words texts and any AI questions and answers
- Your members' access ([number] memberships) and pending invitations
- The accounts of people who used Constitution Intelligence only for [organisation name]
  ([number] accounts). People who also belong to another organisation keep their account; only
  their access to [organisation name] was removed.
- Your portal's branding and logo, and your organisation's activity log

**What we keep, and why**

- Invoices, payments and receipts, and the record of your subscription, because we must keep
  financial records for [period, as advised by our accountant].
- A short record that the deletion happened (when, who approved it, and how many records were
  removed), with no names or contents.

**Backups**: copies of the data remain inside our encrypted backups until they expire, at the
latest on [deletion date + 35 days]. They are not used, and after that date they are gone.

[If an export was provided:] The export we sent you on [date] is now the only copy of your data
outside those backups. Please keep it safe.

If you have any questions, reply to this email or contact [support address].

Kind regards,
[name], Constitution Intelligence
