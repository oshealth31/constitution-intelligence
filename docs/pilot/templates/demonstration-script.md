## Honest answers to likely questions

- **"Does it use AI to answer questions?"** Not in the pilot. AI answering is built but
  switched off because of data-protection rules on sending data abroad. Members get approved
  answers and clause matching instead.
- **"Can it read our scanned constitution?"** Not scanned images. We can type it for you; your
  people check and approve the typed version.
- **"Is it legal advice?"** No. It shows your constitution and explanations your leadership
  approved.
- **"Where is our data kept?"** To be confirmed before the pilot starts (planned: a data centre
  in South Africa or Botswana). Never shared with other organisations.
- **"Can members pay through it?"** No. Invoices are paid by EFT or mobile money and checked by
  hand.
- **"Is there an app?"** It is a website that can be added to a phone's home screen. Tested in
  Chrome; iPhone/Safari not yet tested.
- **"What if we stop?"** You get an export of your data, and it is deleted after the agreed period.
