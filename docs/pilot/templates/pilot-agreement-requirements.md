# Template: pilot agreement requirements

**This is a list of what the pilot agreement must cover, for a lawyer to turn into a contract.
It is not a contract and not legal advice.** Every ⚖️ item needs review by a qualified legal
professional in Botswana (and the tax items by an accountant) before anything is signed.

## Parties and scope
- Constitution Intelligence (legal entity name, registration number, address) and the
  organisation (registered name, registration number, address, authorised signatory and their
  authority to sign) ⚖️ (is CI a registered company? who signs for it?)
- The service: a private online portal for the organisation's constitution: reading, search,
  "Find relevant clauses", organisation-approved answers and simple-words texts, offline
  reading, member management, subscription and invoices. Reference the
  [pilot limitations](pilot-limitations.md) as an annex.
- Excluded: AI answering, legal advice, printing, interpretation of the constitution, dispute
  resolution between members.

## Pilot duration
- 60 days from the portal activation date (written notice of activation).
- What happens at the end: continuation on a paid plan by new agreement or order form, or
  ending with export (and deletion on request).
- Can the pilot be extended, and at what price? ⚖️

## Fees, payment and cancellation ⚖️
- Founding pilot fee (from P1,500; the exact amount per organisation), currency BWP, whether VAT
  is included (accountant).
- Invoice, due within 7 days; payment by EFT or mobile money with the invoice number as
  reference; access is activated when payment is verified.
- Late payment: 7-day grace period, then access is restricted (read-only for Owners and
  Administrators); no data is deleted.
- Cancellation by either party with 7 days' written notice. Refunds (none, pro-rata, or full in
  the first N days?) to decide ⚖️.
- Post-pilot prices: stated, or "to be offered by day 45".

## Ownership
- CI owns the platform and all intellectual property in it, including improvements arising from
  pilot feedback ⚖️.
- The organisation owns its content and grants CI a limited, non-exclusive right to store,
  process and display it only to provide the service to that organisation ⚖️.
- The organisation warrants it may upload the documents and has the authority to approve
  published content.
- Use of the organisation's name or logo as a reference customer only with separate written
  permission.

## Data protection (Data Protection Act, 2024) ⚖️
- Roles: organisation = controller of members' personal data; CI = processor (proposed). Is
  that right for every kind of data, including CI's own billing contacts?
- Processing instructions: what data, purposes, retention, location (hosting in South Africa or
  Botswana; cross-border transfer basis; whether a copy must stay in Botswana).
- Sensitive data: church or faith-based organisations' membership may reveal religious belief.
  Lawful basis and safeguards.
- Security measures (annex: summary of docs/security and docs/operations).
- Breach: CI informs the organisation without undue delay (target 24 hours) so the
  organisation can notify the Information and Data Protection Commission within 72 hours.
- Sub-processors: hosting provider, email provider, any monitoring service; prior notice of
  changes.
- Assistance with members' requests (access, correction, deletion).
- Return and deletion at the end: export within N days, deletion after the retention period,
  backups expiring after 35 days.
- Registration with the Commission, impact assessment, data protection officer: who needs what.

## Service, support and responsibility ⚖️
- Support hours and response targets (see [support process](support-process.md)); no uptime
  guarantee during the pilot ("best effort").
- Organisation responsibilities: named pilot contact, accurate member list, content review and
  approval, members told about the portal and its privacy notice.
- **Not legal advice**: answers and simple-words texts are explanations approved by the
  organisation; the original constitution prevails.
- Limitation of liability and exclusions; cap (for example the fees paid) ⚖️.
- Suspension rights (non-payment, misuse, security emergency).
- Acceptable use (no unlawful content, no sharing accounts, no attempts to break security).
- Confidentiality both ways.
- Governing law (Botswana), disputes, notices (email addresses), changes to the agreement,
  signatures (electronic signatures acceptable?) ⚖️.
