# Template: pilot limitations (to share with every pilot organisation before signing)

Constitution Intelligence is new. During the founding pilot, these limits apply. We will tell
you if any of them changes.

**What you get:** a private, branded portal with your approved constitution; reading by
section; search; "Find relevant clauses"; answers and simple-words texts approved by your
leadership; reading and searching without internet on the same device; roles for owners,
administrators, reviewers, members and auditors; an activity log; invoices and receipts.

**Limits:**
1. **No AI answering.** It is switched off (data-protection reasons). Questions get your approved
   answers or the matching clauses. Nothing is legal advice; your constitution's wording counts.
2. **Your people approve everything** members see. Text extracted from your document may
   contain mistakes until your reviewer checks it. Scanned images cannot be read; we can type the
   document for you to check.
3. **Size:** up to 100 member accounts, 3 documents, 200 MB of files per organisation.
4. **Devices:** tested in Google Chrome on computers and phones (phone screens simulated). Safari
   (iPhone/iPad) and other browsers are not yet tested. Offline reading works per device, needs
   one online visit first, and copies older than 30 days are hidden until they refresh.
5. **Availability:** no uptime guarantee during the pilot; planned maintenance is announced 3
   working days ahead (usually Sunday early morning).
6. **Support** by email/WhatsApp in working hours (see the support process).
7. **Payments** by EFT or mobile money only, checked by hand; access starts once payment is
   verified.
8. **Data:** stored in [location to be confirmed: South Africa or Botswana], encrypted backups
   daily. You can get a full export at any time on request; deletion is done by us on request
   (no self-service deletion).
9. **Sign-in:** email and password. Two-factor sign-in is not available yet; it is planned for
   Constitution Intelligence staff accounts before the pilot starts.
10. **Things still being finished** before or during the pilot are listed in your agreement; we
    do not promise features that are not built.
