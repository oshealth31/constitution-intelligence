# Template: support process (for pilot organisations)

## How to get help

- **Who asks:** your pilot contact (or an Owner/Administrator). Members ask their own
  administrators first, so CI hears about each problem once.
- **How:** email [support address] or WhatsApp [number]. Give: your organisation, what you
  tried, what happened, the time, a screenshot, and the **request ID** if an error shows one.
- **Never send passwords.** Nobody from Constitution Intelligence will ever ask for one.

## When we respond (pilot, best effort)

| Problem            | Examples                                                                          | First response                 | Aim to resolve                              |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Urgent             | Portal down for everyone; members see data that is not theirs; suspected break-in | 4 working hours (call as well) | Same or next working day                    |
| Normal             | Someone cannot sign in; upload fails; wrong text after extraction                 | 1 working day                  | 3 working days                              |
| Question / request | How do I…; new feature idea                                                       | 1 working day                  | Answered; ideas go to the feedback register |

Working hours: Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00 Botswana time, excluding public holidays. No uptime
guarantee during the pilot.

## What we do (internal)

1. Log the request: reference SUP-YYYY-NNN, organisation, contact, severity, description.
2. Investigate with the Control Centre (Support page, Audit log) and the logs (request ID).
   Looking at the organisation's constitution text needs a reason. Enter the SUP reference; the
   access is recorded in the organisation's own activity log, which its Owner can see.
3. Reply with what was found and what happens next; update at least daily for urgent problems.
4. Close the request when the contact confirms; record the cause.
5. Security or personal-data problems follow [incident-response.md](../../operations/incident-response.md)
   immediately (including the 72-hour notification clock).

## What support does not cover

Interpreting the constitution, settling disputes between members, legal advice, changes to
the organisation's content without its approval, and devices or internet connections.
